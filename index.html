FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What makes Pawfect different?
Every product is crafted with premium materials, ethically sourced, and designed with your pet's comfort and safety as the priority. We partner with artisans and sustainable suppliers.
Do you offer international shipping?
Yes, we ship worldwide. Delivery times and costs vary by region. Free shipping is available on orders over $50 within the contiguous US.
What is your return policy?
We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If you're not completely happy with your purchase, we'll arrange a return or exchange at no cost to you.
Are your products safe for pets?
Absolutely. All materials are non-toxic, BPA-free, and rigorously tested. We prioritize safety and comfort in every product we design.
